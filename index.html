01 — About

Engineer → analyst → PM.
Always close to the data.

I started as an IoT engineer and data analyst, training ML models on truck GPS and IoT data. Over nearly seven years at WheelsEye I grew into a Senior Product Manager, working across connected devices, analytics, operations and eventually FASTag and payments for 1.5 million trucks.

At PCL Health, a London healthtech startup, I ran remote-care pilots with NHS hospitals — applying the same curiosity to a very different set of customer needs.

I enjoy understanding customer problems, finding patterns in data, and bringing teams together to turn those insights into useful products. I'm especially interested in how products handle uncertainty: when to automate, when to ask for human judgment, and how to know whether the outcome is actually better.

Now at IIM Ahmedabad's PGPX, I'm building the business perspective to lead larger products and teams. My research explores AI-based credit risk for small businesses.

Not AI for show. AI that solves a specific problem, improves a decision, and earns customers' trust.

  • Products with a model or a score at the core
  • Human-in-the-loop where confidence is low
  • Outcomes measured, not assumed
