Files starting with an underscore are ignored by the site. To add a poem, copy this file,
rename it without the underscore (for example `monsoon.md`), and edit it.

For a Hindi poem set `lang: hi`, write the title and the lines in Devanagari, and add an
optional English line under the title with `english: "..."` in the front matter.

Put each stanza in its own paragraph. A line break inside a stanza needs two spaces at the end of the line.
